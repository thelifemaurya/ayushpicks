# AyushPicks backlink strategy

Goal: earn links because AyushPicks is genuinely useful, not buy them. Paid or spammy links risk a Google penalty.

## 1. Build link-worthy assets first (weeks 1-4)
- Publish 20+ complete product pages with honest pros/limitations and real photos.
- Make 3-5 category "buying guides" (e.g. "Earbuds for gaming under ₹2000") and 3 comparison pages. These are the pages other sites will cite.
- Add an original data point per guide (price ranges, what we tested, what we skipped).

## 2. Easy foundations (week 1)
- Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools; submit /sitemap.xml.
- Profiles with a link and the same name/description: Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Facebook page, Medium, Quora, Reddit (as a person, not spam).
- Business-style listings: Product Hunt launch, BetaList, AlternativeTo, Crunchbase, Indian startup directories.

## 3. Outreach that works (weeks 3-12)
- Share a specific guide with tech/lifestyle bloggers, college clubs and newsletters in India. Offer something useful, not "please link to us".
- HARO-style: Qwoted, Featured.com, SourceBottle, Twitter/X journalist requests; answer with data from your guides.
- Student angle: college blogs and campus communities love "student essentials" lists.
- Creators: send guide links to YouTubers who review the same products; they often link their sources.

## 4. Content partnerships
- Guest posts on relevant blogs (1-2 a month, quality only) with a natural link to one guide.
- Podcasts and creator collabs; ask for a link in the show notes.
- Broken-link building: find dead product/guide links on relevant sites and offer your page as the replacement.

## 5. Rules
- Affiliate links on AyushPicks stay rel="sponsored nofollow" (already set).
- Vary anchor text; mostly brand and page names, not exact keywords.
- Never buy links, join link farms, or trade links in bulk.

## 6. Track
Search Console > Links, plus a free Ahrefs Webmaster Tools or Bing report. Target: 5-10 quality referring domains per month, check for toxic ones quarterly.
